# Community Meetings

Community meetings are open conversations between the people who build DSX AI Factory projects and the people who use them: customers, partners, and developers outside NVIDIA. This page describes how the meetings work and gives a suggested agenda that communities can adapt.

For each community's schedule, notes, and mailing list, see the [communities list](README.md).

## How meetings work

- **Open to everyone**, under the [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- **Announced in advance** on the community's mailing list and on the [DSX AI Factory Community calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed?src=c_d83d62cba35a7b67c739c701dd77122feefff93389c4cc83b5c2b1d224c07c75%40group.calendar.google.com).
- **A public agenda.** Each community has a meeting notes document that anyone can comment on. Add your topic or question before the meeting.
- **Public notes.** Notes are kept in the meeting notes document. Recordings of online meetings, and any slides, go in the community's public Drive folder.
- **Discussion continues** in the community's Slack channel and on its mailing list.

## Suggested agenda

Communities can adapt this agenda to fit their meeting.

1. **Welcome** - introductions for anyone new, and a reminder that the meeting is public and follows the Code of Conduct.
2. **Community updates** - recent releases, planned work, and anything that needs feedback.
3. **Topics from the agenda** - questions and items added to the meeting notes document.
4. **Show and tell** - a short demo from anyone in the community: something you built, or how you run the software.
5. **Wrap-up** - next steps, and the date of the next meeting.

## Adding a meeting to the calendar

Meeting invites go to the community's mailing list, so anyone who joins the list receives them. To add a new meeting series to the DSX AI Factory Community calendar, ask in the DSX AI Factory Slack workspace.
